This is a working model of the design now in flutter_app_v2. Same tokens, same rule for the vessel, same type ladder. Move the phone — or use the sliders — and the liquid finds its own level while the order notch stays on the glass.

The sliders stand in for the gyro so this page works on a desktop. On the phone, dragging sideways anywhere on the screen leans the vessel by thumb, and the sensor does it hands-free. Release and the liquid finds its level again — it does not stay where you left it.

What changed from the mockup

1. The threshold can no longer lie. The notch is a ring painted on the glass at world height limit ÷ capacity, so when the liquid tips, the line tips with it. A line pinned to the screen was the one thing that would have made the whole gesture untrustworthy.
2. The liquid is data. It is drawn in the stock state's colour at real strength, and the cool glass wash sits on top of it. In the mockup the liquid was decorative cyan while a pulsing red border carried the warning — the vessel would glow when everything was fine.
3. "Synced" is earned. The pill reads the pending outbox and says 3 WAITING when there is work to upload. The old pulsing dot claimed a state it never checked.
4. Gyro cost stays flat. One sensor subscription per shell, and samples repaint vessels only — no screen rebuilds at 30 Hz. In the app that is an InheritedNotifier, not a ref.watch.
5. Ten pixels is the floor. The mockup had 9–9.5 px labels everywhere; here nothing readable is under 10, quantities are 17, and formulas are mono so HCl can't read as HCI.
6. Five tabs, not three. Apparatus and reports keep their place; only the skin changed.

Vessel rule, as implemented

level  quantity ÷ max(initialQuantity, quantity), clamped 0–1
notch  limit ÷ capacity, pinned to the top when the limit exceeds the bottle
state  zero → berry · at or below limit → clay · otherwise sage
no limit  reads fine and is never counted as needing attention — except at zero
ticks  five segments, rounded down, minimum one when anything is left

Browser note: iOS only grants motion access after a tap, which is what the gyro checkbox is for. Nothing on this page needs it — the sliders and the drag are the same maths.
